Tune Match

Press play to hear a short tune, then choose the option that feels closest. Play it again as many times as you like.

Take your time. There is no clock running.

About Tune Match

Tune Match is a free online music game for adults living with early-stage dementia or mild memory changes. Each of the five rounds plays a short, gentle melody generated in the browser — nothing to download — and asks which of three moods it feels closest to: a calm morning, a quiet evening, or a lively dance. Music often reaches people warmly even when words are harder, and this round asks only for a feeling, so there is no right answer to get wrong. You can replay the tune as many times as you like, and if sound is unavailable a short written description of the melody appears instead. A full game takes about five minutes.
