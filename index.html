The thinking behind it

Why these activities may help

These games are inspired by cognitive stimulation approaches that are often used, informally and in structured programmes, with people living with early-stage dementia. Each one leans on something familiar — everyday objects, simple categories, or a gentle tune — rather than testing memory under pressure.

🔎 Recognition, not recall

Memory Lane uses familiar, concrete images so the activity feels like noticing, not straining to remember.

🗂️ Simple categorisation

Find the Group draws on everyday groupings — fruits, kitchen items, clothing — that tend to stay familiar for a long time.

🎶 Music and reminiscence

Tune Match uses short, gentle melodies. Music and familiar cues are often associated with calm engagement.

🌿 Confidence, not competition

There are no scores to chase and no timers to beat. The aim is a pleasant few minutes, not a test.

A note on what this is — and isn’t

This site offers supportive, wellness-oriented activities for enjoyment and gentle engagement. For families and caregivers

A short guide to using YoursCalmly

Many people come here looking for a simple, calming online activity to share with a parent, partner, or friend living with early-stage dementia or mild cognitive changes. This section explains what the games involve, how long a session takes, and how to introduce them gently.

What a session actually looks like

A round asks one clear question — "Which one is used for making a cup of tea?" — and offers a few large picture choices. There are five rounds in each game, and a full game takes roughly five to ten minutes at an unhurried pace. You can stop after a single round; nothing is lost, and nothing needs to be finished. Choosing an unexpected answer produces a warm, matter-of-fact response rather than a buzzer or a red cross.

How to introduce the games

Sitting alongside someone and letting them tap for themselves usually works better than handing over a device with instructions. Open one game rather than presenting all three at once, read the question aloud if that helps, and let silence sit — the screen will wait indefinitely. If a round feels frustrating, moving to a different game, or stopping entirely, is a perfectly good outcome. The aim is a pleasant few minutes together, not a completed set.

Choosing which game to open first

Memory Lane is the gentlest starting point: three familiar pictures, one simple question, one tap. Find the Group asks for a little more — picking several pictures that belong to the same everyday category, such as fruits or kitchen items — and suits someone who enjoys sorting things. Tune Match plays a short melody and asks which mood it fits; it works well for people who respond warmly to music, and it can be played by ear alone, with a written description available if sound is unavailable.

What this site is not

YoursCalmly does not assess, score, track, or diagnose anything. There is no account, no caregiver dashboard, no analytics, and no data leaves the device. It is not a substitute for a clinical cognitive stimulation programme, occupational therapy, or medical advice. If you have questions about memory, thinking, or a diagnosis, a qualified healthcare professional is the right place to start.

Built with care

Accessibility and design principles

These choices were made on purpose, to keep every screen calm and easy to use.

☝️ One thing per screen

Every screen asks for a single action, so there is never a question of what to do next.

🔠 Large text and targets

Body text is large and buttons are big enough to tap comfortably, even with less steady hands.

🌗 Strong, warm contrast

Colours are chosen for readability first, with warm tones instead of harsh clinical brights.

⌨️ Keyboard and screen-reader friendly

Every button and card can be reached and used without a mouse, with clear focus outlines throughout.

⏳ No countdown pressure

Nothing is timed by default. Rounds wait patiently for as long as they are needed.

🍃 Calm motion

Animations are minimal and respect your device’s reduced-motion setting.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the games free to play?

Yes. All three YoursCalmly games are completely free, with no account, sign-up, subscription, or app download required — just open the site in a browser and start playing.

Who are these games designed for?

YoursCalmly is designed for adults living with early-stage dementia or mild memory changes who enjoy playing on their own, as well as caregivers and family members looking for a calm activity to share.

Is YoursCalmly a medical treatment for dementia?

No. YoursCalmly offers supportive, wellness-oriented activities for enjoyment and gentle engagement. It is not a medical device, treatment, or diagnostic tool, and does not claim to cure, prevent, or slow the progression of dementia or any condition.

Do the games have timers or scores?

No. None of the three games use countdown timers or competitive scoring by default. Every round waits patiently for as long as it is needed, so there is no pressure to hurry or perform.

Can I play in Chinese?

Yes. Use the language toggle at the top of any page to switch the entire site — including all three games — between English and Chinese instantly, with no reload.

What devices can I play on?

YoursCalmly works in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer, with large text and large buttons designed to be easy to read and tap, even with less steady hands.
